How farms are refined

Sowing. Sown share = sown pixels' overlap area ÷ farm area. ≥ 70% → whole farm sown; ≤ 30% → not sown; in between → partially sown, area kept proportional. Progress is cumulative.

Acreage. Class areas come from exact pixel overlap inside each farm. Dominant class ≥ 70% → single-class, whole farm area to that class. Otherwise multi-class: classes under 10% dropped, farm area split between the rest by share (marked * and dashed outline).

Survey numbers are generated sample values, not official land records.
